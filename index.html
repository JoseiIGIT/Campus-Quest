          A QUICK GUIDE

          How Campus Quest works

          1. Pick a quest. Try the Freshman Campus Tour, the Visitor Welcome Path or a quick side quest.
          2. Walk to the glowing spot. The app uses your phone's location. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.
          3. Stay for the timer. Each stop has a minimum stay. Leave the area for more than 10 seconds and the timer restarts. Enjoy the campus story while you wait.
          4. Take 1 photo. Your photo is your proof. Attach it and complete the stop to earn XP and badges.
